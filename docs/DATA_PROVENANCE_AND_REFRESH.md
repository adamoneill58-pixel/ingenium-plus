# Data provenance and refresh

## Classification

- **Verified:** transcribed or derived from an identified official source and checked on the recorded verification date.
- **Calculated:** generated by INGENIUM+ from documented inputs, such as date status, graph degree, profile completion or recommendation components.
- **Sample:** fictional/demo content used to demonstrate product behaviour. Sample students and chats are never real-person claims.

These classes must not be collapsed. “Verified” describes source evidence, not institutional approval of INGENIUM+.

## Catalogue lineage

v1.4 and v1.5 maintain a checked-in catalogue and evidence ledger. The v1.5.1 seed generator converts that sanitised catalogue into idempotent SQL. The production schema is `SCHEMA.sql`; `SEED.sql` populates the public catalogue projection, relationships, evidence sources, partner organisations, semantic features, refresh schedules and dataset marker. Personal profiles, memberships, submissions, private documents and audit history are created at runtime and are excluded from this archive.

## Refresh model

Each schedule points to an evidence source and records cadence, enablement and next-run state. A refresh run fetches the official source conditionally where supported, applies timeouts and bounded retries, hashes/snapshots the response and records the outcome. A detected change becomes a proposed change for review; it is not silently published.

Manual refresh is available through the authorised Staff/Data Health path. No cloud scheduler is currently attached, so database schedule rows do not execute themselves. A production owner must attach an approved scheduler/automation, monitor failures and retain the human review gate.

## Rules

- Prefer official INGENIUM and partner-university sources.
- Never fabricate academic information, eligibility, recognition, deadlines or official status.
- Preserve source URLs, verification dates and ambiguity notes.
- Treat expired deadlines from dates, even when a stale source label says “open”.
- Do not scrape or publish real student identities.
- Keep official application, enrolment, recognition and record systems authoritative.
