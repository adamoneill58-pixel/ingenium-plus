# INGENIUM catalogue review — 6 November 2026

The public release grows from 222 to 243 records: 21 additions and 68 existing records refreshed. The review used 104 successful page fetches, including the nine-page course index, all 51 listed course detail pages, 13 BIP event pages, current calls and dated initiative announcements. The release includes 90 new source-ledger entries. The JSON file alongside this note lists each changed record, changed fields and reviewed source URLs.

## Main changes

- Added the four November microcredentials, with pre-registration windows, credits, capacity and the €35 fee. Only Climate Change and Environmental Toxicology is accepting pre-registration on the review date. The other windows open on 8, 9 and 10 November.
- Added Skövde’s three autumn sustainability microcredentials, the Irish language course and the clinical simulation learning unit. Existing course records now reflect the official index’s registration status and the detail pages’ published fields. BIP virtual components link to existing BIPs rather than creating duplicate programmes.
- Confirmed Student Partnerships final applications run from 4 November to 1 December, 23:59 CET; the optional EOI stage has ended. Added the rolling local student event call (up to €3,000), the student film call, Community Days and five online events, including the 10 November matchmaking session.
- Added the Annual Education Call EOI deadline of 13 November, retaining 18 December for full proposals. Its 31 Fast Track submissions are described as proposals, not approved courses.
- Updated Ambassadors and Student Conference records with selection results. Updated PhD scholarship outcomes, the closed 2026 doctoral intake and the CBPT degree-label announcement. Added the Earth and Planetary Sciences doctorate, Rouen’s EDI Charter and the completed Sustainability Week.
- Corrected BIP physical dates and removed stale Digital Health virtual dates. Repaired the broken former course-index URL. Homepage features now select open opportunities and stop featuring calls after their deadlines.

## Uncertainty retained

The four new microcredential starts are one day earlier in the course index than in the dated 4 November announcement. The announcement’s dates are shown with a confirmation note and approximate activity precision, preventing exact activity calendar exports. Their pre-registration dates agree with the detailed course pages.

Digital Health and SeismicX contain conflicting delivery dates. The older provisional Rouen Inclusion BIP overview links to a separate programme in Oviedo. Those entries retain verification flags and are not presented as open calls. Local event funding has an inconsistent October opening date within its page; both versions establish that the rolling round is already open.

This was a targeted review, not a claim that every historical record or every partner-university website was fully revalidated. Earlier evidence dates remain on untouched records. Official systems and local university selection rules continue to decide admission, recognition and funding.
