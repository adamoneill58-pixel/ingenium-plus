# INGENIUM catalogue review for 16 November 2026

The reviewed catalogue contains 251 public records. This refresh adds eight records and updates 14 existing records after the early correction to the Annual Education Call. The companion JSON file records changed fields and source links.

- Three Xamk online courses have January 2027 intakes with enrolment through 16 December. The spring intakes have their own pages and the autumn records are retained. [Official course catalogue](https://ingenium-university.eu/courses/)
- The Nature-based Wellbeing in Social Care microcredential lists pre-registration from 14 December to 18 January, 2 ECTS, a fee waiver for INGENIUM students and a €35 fee for other participants. [Official course details](https://ingenium-university.eu/courses/nature-based-social-care/)
- Rouen confirmed the Joint European Degree Label approval for the CBPT master's programme and published a February 2027 taster BIP in Iași. The Rouen notice asks applicants to submit before 2 December, which makes 1 December the last full day before the stated date. Funding and application arrangements are scoped to the published Rouen route. [Rouen announcement](https://www.univ-rouen.fr/actualites/label-europeen-de-diplome-conjoint-pour-le-master-cbpt-dingenium/)
- The Oviedo Student Conference and the Final Conference have separate records. The Final Conference entry links to the official programme and keeps access arrangements unconfirmed. The official calendar and some session descriptions disagree on timezone labels, so the catalogue records the date and venue for the conference. [Student Conference update](https://ingenium-university.eu/50-students-selected-for-the-first-ingenium-student-conference/) · [November programme](https://ingenium-university.eu/events/month/2026-11/)
- The Stories on Screen listing now describes the original film and official clip routes. The Community Days review also added the past intercultural workshop and updated the student mobility panel's sources. The November chess tournament is retained as a past event. [Film call](https://ingenium-university.eu/ingenium-stories-on-screen-community-days-initiative/) · [Community Days at TUIASI](https://www.tuiasi.ro/evenimente/universitatea-tehnica-gheorghe-asachi-din-iasi-va-invita-la-seria-de-evenimente-ingenium-community-days-2026-organizate-online-si-onsite-in-perioada-3-20-noiembrie/)

[Current official timeline](https://ingenium-university.eu/iec-faculty/ingenium-annual-education-call-2026-2027/) · [Earlier news post](https://ingenium-university.eu/ingenium-receives-31-fast-track-proposals-as-the-annual-education-call-26-27-continues/)
