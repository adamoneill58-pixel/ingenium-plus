# INGENIUM+ v1.5 — Authoritative Build Specification

This document is the authoritative specification for INGENIUM+ v1.5.

Codex should:
- Read this document in full before implementation.
- Treat it as the primary source of truth for v1.5 requirements.
- Inspect the existing v1.4 codebase before changing architecture.
- Preserve working functionality unless this specification explicitly changes it.
- Implement the release incrementally and test after each major phase.
- Clearly distinguish verified, calculated, and sample data.
- Update this document only if explicitly asked to change the specification.

# INGENIUM+ v1.5 — MASTER BUILD PROMPT

You are upgrading the existing **INGENIUM+ v1.4** application into **INGENIUM+ v1.5**.

Current deployed reference:

**https://ingenium-plus-v14.a0n720x.chatgpt.site/?mode=student**

Do **not** rebuild the project from scratch.

This is an evolution of the existing product. Preserve what already works, preserve its visual identity, preserve its core graph-first concept, and improve/refactor where necessary.

The purpose of v1.5 is not simply to add more pages.

The purpose is to make INGENIUM+ feel like a **substantially more complete European digital campus**, where learning, mobility, opportunities, projects, communities, students and communication are all connected through one underlying network.

The central product principle is:

> **The graph is not a feature inside INGENIUM+. The graph is INGENIUM+.**

Everything else should connect back to it.

# 2. PRODUCT VISION

INGENIUM+ is a graph-first digital layer for the INGENIUM European University Alliance.

The long-term vision is that a student should be able to understand and eventually navigate an entire European university ecosystem through one connected interface.

Relevant entities include:

- universities;
- students;
- programmes;
- modules;
- courses;
- microcredentials;
- BIPs;
- mobility opportunities;
- events;
- communities;
- student projects;
- research initiatives;
- entrepreneurship initiatives;
- sustainability initiatives;
- platforms;
- learning resources;
- skills where relevant;
- organisations;
- applications;
- pathways.

Relationships are as important as the individual records.

The user should constantly be able to answer:

> What is this?

> Why is it relevant to me?

> What is it connected to?

> Who else is involved?

> What can I do next?

# 3. PRODUCT POSITIONING

INGENIUM+ should feel like a mixture of:

**European academic campus + network discovery platform + lightweight student social layer.**

It should NOT feel like:

- LinkedIn;
- Discord;
- a conventional university website;
- a generic SaaS dashboard;
- a social-media feed;
- a static course catalogue.

The academic campus remains the core.

The social features emerge naturally from participation in modules, BIPs, projects and communities.

The long-term direction should support INGENIUM becoming increasingly experienced by students as **one European university**, rather than ten disconnected institutions.

The graph should become the dominant feature of the homepage.

Keep the title and proposition, but make the hero significantly smaller.

**Personalised Actionable Now**

# 6. HOMEPAGE GRAPH SIZE

On desktop, target approximately:

**70% of the viewport height**

for the graph workspace.

It should feel substantial.

Do not place it inside a tiny card.

Allow it to use most of the available page width.

The graph should immediately communicate:

> This network is the central interface of INGENIUM+.

On mobile, adapt intelligently rather than simply scaling the desktop graph down.

# 7. FIX THE GEOGRAPHIC MAP COMPLETELY

The current graph/map has a major immersion problem.

For example, dragging west past Ireland eventually exposes white space because the map background ends.

This must be fixed.

The geographic canvas should feel effectively continuous within the meaningful browsing area.

Users should be able to:

- pan left;
- pan right;
- pan up;
- pan down;
- zoom;
- zoom out;

without exposing blank white space or visible map boundaries.

Use an appropriate technical approach:

- proper geographic tiles;
- a sufficiently large SVG/world map;
- wrapped map projection;
- map overscan;
- constrained but natural camera bounds;
- or another robust solution.

Do not simply enlarge the current image.

The solution must work across screen sizes.

The geography and graph must behave as one coordinate system.

# 8. TWO GRAPH MODES

Create two clearly different graph modes.

## GEOGRAPHY MODE

Geography matters.

University nodes are fixed approximately to their real geographic positions.

For example:

- MTU in Ireland;
- HKA in Germany;
- Xamk in Finland;
- University of Crete in Greece;
- etc.

Other entities should cluster around their relevant university or universities.

Examples:

MTU

↳ hosted modules

↳ programmes

↳ opportunities

↳ projects

↳ BIPs

↳ student initiatives

Some records associated with several universities can sit appropriately between or around them.

Geography Mode helps answer:

> Where does this happen?

---

## NETWORK MODE

Geographic position no longer controls the layout.

Use a freer force-directed graph.

Relationships become the primary visual structure.

Nodes should cluster based on meaningful connections, themes and shared participation.

Network Mode helps answer:

> How is this connected?

The same underlying dataset must power both modes.

Do not create two disconnected graph systems.

- direct relationships remain strongly visible;

1. locate it;
2. centre it;

# 14. REAL DATA VS SAMPLE DATA

Some infrastructure does not yet exist.

However, three information categories must remain clearly distinguishable.

## VERIFIED

Real information from official sources.

## INGENIUM+ CALCULATED

Information derived by the application.

Examples:

- relevance score;
- connection degree;
- ECTS total;
- recommendations.

## SAMPLE

Information created solely to demonstrate functionality.

Examples:

- sample students;
- sample chat messages;
- sample enrolment status;

**Sample information**

# 30. CONNECTION DEGREE SYSTEM

Student-to-student relationships should have degrees calculated from shared participation and connections.

Do not manually hard-code degrees where they can be derived.

## FIRST DEGREE

Students have:

**explicitly connected**

AND

have shared participation in at least one meaningful activity.

Examples:

- shared module;
- shared BIP;
- shared project;
- shared programme;
- shared community;
- shared event.

Example:

> Sofia  
> **1st**  
> Connected · You both completed Academic Communication

---

## SECOND DEGREE

Students share meaningful participation but are not explicitly connected.

Example:

> Maria  
> **2nd**  
> You both participated in CLARITY-AI

This creates natural opportunities to discover people through academic life.

---

## THIRD DEGREE

Students do not necessarily share an activity, but share one or more connections.

Example:

> Lukas  
> **3rd**  
> 3 shared connections

Where useful, show which mutual connections explain the relationship.

---

# FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Do not judge the success of v1.5 by how many pages were added.

Judge it by whether the entire product now feels connected.

A module should connect to a university.

A module should connect to its students.

Those students should connect to one another.

The module should connect to related opportunities.

The university should connect to programmes and projects.

A BIP should connect institutions, students and themes.

A student's participation should visibly expand their network.

Their My Campus should be built from those relationships.

Their recommendations should emerge from those relationships.

Their Chats should exist because of those relationships.

Their academic journey should become a graph.

The ultimate goal is to make INGENIUM feel less like:

> **ten universities collaborating**

and more like:

> **one European university made visible through its connections.**

That is **INGENIUM+ v1.5**.
