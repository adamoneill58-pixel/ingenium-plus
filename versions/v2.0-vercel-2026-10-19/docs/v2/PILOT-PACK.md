## Facilitator task script

Explain that this is a prototype and official pages decide eligibility, enrolment, funding and credits. Ask participants not to enter personal information. Do not require an account. Use fictional course information for the academic task. Record task observations separately only under the approved evaluation handling; application event counts cannot track an individual funnel.

1. In one sentence, describe what the homepage appears to help with.
2. Find an opportunity outside your home university in an area you care about. Explain why it may suit you and follow its official route.
3. Assume you cannot travel. Find an online option and identify any eligibility or funding fact still needing confirmation.
4. Use List and Network for the same filters. Explain one evidenced connection. Use the Map to identify a host without assuming it is an online course venue.
5. Move the same search to Calendar. Find a future application date, inspect the home-university process and export a reminder. Explain the timezone uncertainty and snapshot limitation. Check a conflicting example and explain why export is unavailable.
6. Save two items, compare practical details, inspect upcoming actions and export a temporary plan. Explain what refresh will lose and who decides credit recognition.
7. Academic demonstration: enter a fictional AI/sustainable-cities course, review suggested tags, compare complementary expertise and draft an expression of interest. Confirm that no real profile/contact is used and nothing is sent.
8. Complete the thirteen-question survey preview only when the approved collection environment is active. Survey wording is defined in `lib/v2/survey.ts` from the current Drive brief.

Observe success, hesitation, misleading labels, missing data and view preference. Avoid collecting names/emails or screen recordings without a separate approved purpose and handling. Do not turn staff test activity into pilot participation evidence.
