# INGENIUM+ v1.4 release notes

Research freeze: **5 August 2026**

## Headline change

v1.4 turns the graph prototype into a complete student discovery product. The graph remains the homepage centrepiece, but it now sits inside a clearly defined dark “network stage” and is supported by actionable opportunities, guided journeys, focused directories and a private local planner.

## New

- 85 source-backed entities and 343 typed relationships across ten canonical universities.
- Current live calls for the CBPT Joint Master’s, Human-Centred AI doctorate and doctoral mobility scholarships.
- Six guided routes: BIPs, European Pathways, sustainability, entrepreneurship, digital learning and 10 Days.
- Multi-mode Cytoscape graph with pan, zoom, search, filters, URL state, selectable relationships and an accessible list.
- Progressive-disclosure journey steps so large routes remain complete without crowding the screen.
- Searchable/filterable pages for opportunities, programmes, events, communities, projects/innovation, platforms and universities.
- My Journey: local saves, ordered steps, personal status, private notes and JSON export.
- Source ledger, evidence block on graph details, confidence model and explicit verification date.
- Complete data-integrity, type, lint, build and server-route test suite.
- Official local INGENIUM logo assets and a restrained abstract social-sharing card.

## Corrected from v1.3

- University relationships are no longer silently truncated.
- The University of Oviedo identifier is canonicalised as `university-uniovi`.
- HS/HIS are aliases of one university, not separate records.
- All four 2026 10 Days schools are labelled completed.
- INGENIUM+ and ResQDrone no longer inherit the funded-project deadline.
- Programme development and application status are visibly separate.
- The BIP host geography reflects all verified hosts in the frozen catalogue.
- Platform and community pages no longer imply a fictional public group-chat product.

## Privacy and scope

No accounts, personal profiles, member directories or private student information are included. Planner data stays in the browser. Official systems remain authoritative for eligibility, applications, enrolment and records.
