## Data policy

Each public graph record in `data.js` contains an official source and `lastVerifiedAt` date. Information not supported by a public official source is omitted or described as unverified.

Exact local BIP application deadlines are not inferred. Students are directed to their home university for local selection, Erasmus+ funding and travel arrangements.

No individual student, private group chat, personal contact detail or participant list is included.
