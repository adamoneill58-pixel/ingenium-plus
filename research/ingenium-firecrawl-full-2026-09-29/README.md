# INGENIUM full Firecrawl research expansion

Research cut-off: **1 October 2026**

This dossier records the exhaustive expansion behind `lib/research-exhaustive-2026-09-29.ts`. It supplements the earlier bounded refreshes rather than replacing their evidence.

## Scope searched

- the central INGENIUM domain;
- all ten partner-university domains, including local-language material;
- current and historical calls, programmes, BIPs, courses, events, communities and platforms;
- research, innovation, entrepreneurship, sustainability, inclusion, wellbeing and digital infrastructure;
- alliance governance, professional roles, partner organisations and the developing EGTC;
- public deliverables and artifacts across WP1–WP10;
- European Commission, CORDIS, Erasmus+, Horizon Europe, European Degree and EUA context;
- chronology from the alliance’s formation through planned 2028 activity.

## Evidence rules

1. Prefer a dated official detail page over a search snippet or stale index label.
2. Treat planned targets, proposals and implementation claims separately from completed outcomes.
3. Preserve conflicting versions, counts and dates rather than silently selecting the more convenient claim.
4. Store a canonical source URL and verification date for each integrated record.
5. Exclude personal email addresses and other unnecessary personal contact data.
6. Use public names only when they evidence a professional, project or governance role.
7. Do not infer missing budgets, participant lists, grant identifiers or operational maturity.

## Integrated result

After this tranche, the live dataset contains:

- **226 records**
- **1,007 typed relationships**
- **159 evidence sources**

The generated SQLite seed is derived from the same data and must report the same counts.

## Main unresolved gaps

- A complete authoritative public deliverable register does not exist; WP coverage is reconstructed from the strategic resources page, progress reports and direct document URLs.
- Public evidence for D1.1, D2.3+, D3.2, D4.2+, D5.2, D5.6, D6.2+, D7.3 and later WP deliverables remains missing or partial.
- The central Pathway page claims 12 active routes but renders only a partial public catalogue. Partner pages prove additional routes, but not a complete canonical register.
- The course catalogue exposed 40 distinct detail pages rather than the expected 36; all 40 are retained, with absent fields left unset.
- The central event research recovered 12 canonical detail records and 50 archive/index URLs, but the archive's presentation does not provide a single complete structured export.
- The official 10 Days index supports seven numbered editions, not ten; the phrase “10 Days” must not be misread as ten editions.
- The EGTC registration was reported in progress in Finland; a final legal-registration notice was not verified by the cut-off.
- The Virtual Student Registry appears in navigation and planning material, but public production evidence remains insufficient.
- Public technical material does not provide a complete partner-by-partner SSO, security, uptime or service-level matrix.
- Several local application deadlines are set by home universities and cannot safely be replaced with one alliance-wide deadline.
- BI4E’s consortium contains eight higher-education institutions, while INGENIUM itself has ten partners.

## Important conflicts retained

- Annual Education Call legacy fragments conflict with its current timeline. The live record uses 16 October, 13 November and 18 December 2026 while retaining a warning.
- D3.3 has filename, internal-version and later-listing inconsistencies.
- D3.5 is described as repository v2 in the progress register but appears as v3 in the public filename.
- D7.1 is reused for the SDG/EGD agenda and several separate sustainability guidance documents.
- D5.4 and D5.5 are labelled inconsistently across the progress report and strategic-resource index.
- Project pages and platform pages frequently describe services as operational without publishing independent production, uptake or security evidence.

## Final Firecrawl execution note

Two bounded bulk crawls returned HTTP 504 after Firecrawl had processed and charged pages server-side. Because their response bodies were not recoverable, no claim in the product relies on those failed crawl outputs. The integrated evidence comes from completed structured-agent results and individually retrievable official pages. Firecrawl activity was stopped when delayed usage accounting moved beyond the displayed included balance; no plan upgrade, auto-reload or credit purchase was authorised.

## Product interpretation

The product should surface verified routes and resources while keeping an explicit `Verification required`, `Access unverified`, `Under development` or `Planned` state where the evidence does not prove a live service or opportunity. Official application pages and each student’s home-university process remain authoritative.
